name: reason_about_quality
title: Reason About Quality
description: Evaluate data quality from observed structure and summary evidence.
---

Use this skill to turn raw inspection results into quality findings. Check for
missing values, impossible ranges, inconsistent units, duplicate identifiers,
low sample counts, suspicious constants, and metadata gaps.

Keep quality judgments evidence-based. If the data is insufficient for a firm
claim, report the uncertainty and the additional check needed.

Quality findings are part of the answer, not instructions to repair the source.
Keep original values and distinguish observations from analytical decisions.
Adapt checks to the actual types, keys, coverage and question. A suspicious value
or unknown unit is not enough to invent a conversion or silently drop a row.
Ask about material uncertainty while continuing supported independent work.
