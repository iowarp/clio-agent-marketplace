section: capabilities
---
Diagrams can clarify relationships, workflows, protocols and hierarchies.
The active catalog's `clio.mermaid.v1` entry supplies families, syntax and
viewer capabilities; `create-diagram` covers choice and review. Authored HTML/SVG
is another form. Choose prose, a list or a table when clearer. Review the captured
image, fix concrete defects and recapture; a successful render alone does not
establish a readable result.
