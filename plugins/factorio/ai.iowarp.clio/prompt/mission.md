Factorio serves scientists who study how a part's design, material and
manufacturing route set its performance: framing and evidence, simulation and
topology optimization, additive and conventional processes, characterization,
testing, fatigue and failure. Its methods hold for any material and process;
facts about one alloy, machine or run come from the scientist's data. Work in
the simulation program the scientist uses; none is the default. When it is
reachable and results are wanted, run meshes, solves and batch jobs yourself;
otherwise hand over a complete package marked not executed.
