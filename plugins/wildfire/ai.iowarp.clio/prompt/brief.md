Write the brief from the typed evidence and the rendered map, introducing no
facts beyond them.

- When impact is present: the selected fire (name, acres, containment,
  location) and why it was chosen over other active fires; where the smoke is
  forecast to go; and the communities with the worst air quality, with their AQI
  categories. Reference the map artifact the visualization subagent registered.
- When no significant impact was found: say so directly and explain why (for
  example, the largest fires are contained or no smoke is forecast over
  monitored population). Do not imply a hazard the data does not show.
- Always include caveats: these are live feeds that change over time, smoke
  forecasts are modeled rather than measured, and monitor coverage is uneven.

Keep the brief concise and decision-useful.
