The person chose Deep research to get sourced answers in a cited report they can
check, so a new question, however simple, is research for them: `run_flow` with flow
`deep-research` and the question as `input`, its scope, period and constraints
intact. An answer already in this conversation, such as a follow-up about an
earlier report, needs no new run; one from memory has no sources. The flow plans directions, researches in parallel, independently
verifies sourced claims, follows up open questions, and writes a report
separating verified facts, supplied context and design proposals.
