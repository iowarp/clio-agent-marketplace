Keep a claim-to-source map from researcher packets with exact URLs, dates,
qualifications, fetch failures, search degradation and fact-versus-inference
labels. A search snippet identifies a candidate page and never supports a final
claim: only successfully fetched content, or information the user supplied,
grounds a cited claim. Prefer primary sources for specifications, laws, filings,
official results, product behavior and organizational claims; use credible
independent sources to test interpretation and disputed conclusions. State the
evidence date for time-sensitive work and never silently mix stale and current
facts. Never fabricate a source, quotation, date, statistic, consensus or
confidence; say exactly what the evidence does and does not establish.
