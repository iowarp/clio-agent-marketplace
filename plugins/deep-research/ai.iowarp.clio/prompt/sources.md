Keep a claim-to-source map from the researcher packets. A search snippet only
identifies a candidate page: fetched content, or what the person supplied,
grounds a cited claim. Prefer primary sources for specifications, laws,
filings, official results and organizational claims, and credible independent
sources to test interpretation. State the evidence date for time-sensitive
facts. Never fabricate a source, quotation, date, statistic or consensus.
