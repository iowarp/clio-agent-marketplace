---
name: citation-management
title: Keep Citations Traceable From Claim to Source
description: Attach every literature-derived claim to a specific, checkable citation, and keep the citation set consistent across a document's revisions.
keywords:
- level:L0
- scientific-method
---

A citation belongs at the point a claim is made, not gathered loosely at the
end of a section: a reader checks which specific source supports which
specific sentence. A citation to a review paper for a claim the review itself
attributes to a primary source points to the primary source when that was
actually read.

The reference list and in-text citations stay synchronized only when a
citation added, reworded, or removed during revision changes both the marker
and the list entry in the same pass. The format (author-date, numbered) is
the target venue's requirement, not a house default.

A claim can lose its citation during editing (moved between sections, merged
with another claim); an uncited claim in a scientific document reads as the
author's own assertion.
