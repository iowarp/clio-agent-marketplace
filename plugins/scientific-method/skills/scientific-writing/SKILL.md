title: Turn an Investigation Into a Written Scientific Deliverable
description: Structure a thesis section, report, or manuscript so every claim traces to its evidence and method, matched to the venue's expected form.
keywords:

---

A thesis section, a journal manuscript, and an internal report carry different
expected sections and levels of methodological detail, but all share the same
requirement: every claim in the text traces to a method and a piece of evidence
presented earlier in the same document.

Results and discussion are distinct: results state what was observed/measured
without interpretation, discussion states what it means and its limits. A
result section that asserts a mechanism, or a discussion section that
introduces a number never reported as a result, breaks that distinction.

A reader deciding whether the work applies to their case needs the boundary
conditions where the relevant claim is made, not only in a closing paragraph.
