title: Identify Genuine Gaps in the Existing Literature
description: Distinguish a real research gap from an under-searched question, and state precisely what evidence would close it.
keywords:
- level:L0
- scientific-method
---

A gap is a claim about the literature, not about memory: it holds only after
`paper-search` has covered the relevant terms, synonyms, and adjacent
literatures and still found nothing, and the search performed and its scope are
what support it.

Three different situations get called "gaps": no prior work exists on the
question; prior work exists but does not cover the specific material,
condition, or scale in question; and prior work is contradictory or
inconclusive. Each implies a different next step (novel contribution,
bounded-scope extension, or a study designed to resolve the contradiction).

A gap analysis that ends at "more research is needed" without naming the
specific missing measurement or comparison, and a method that could produce
it, is not actionable.
