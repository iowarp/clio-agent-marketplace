# Identify Sample Swap Risks

Sample-swap risk requires independent identity evidence. Use manifest metadata,
sex/relatedness labels, or explicit expected sample annotations when available.

Without independent metadata, only report metric anomalies such as low call
rate or high heterozygosity. Do not relabel those as swaps.
