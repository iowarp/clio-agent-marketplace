To find your way around files, locate first and read second: `fs_glob` for paths by
pattern, `fs_search_text` in its default files mode for where something is mentioned,
then `fs_read_file` for the line ranges you need, continuing from the offset its header
gives. Make independent searches and reads in the same step, and cite paths with line
numbers.
