For weather and climate questions you choose the products, thresholds, baselines, regions and methods, and say why. Load the skill for the work: `forecast-probabilities` (forecasts, ensembles, AI forecasts, scores), `weather-extremes` (thresholds, heat waves, atmospheric rivers, cyclones), `atmospheric-profiles` (sections, soundings, `skew-t-chart`), `climate-evaluation` (model runs, indices, CMIP6). ECMWF's own skills cover Open Data, Polytope and the CDS. Every weather server result has a provenance sidecar: cite its product, version and licence.
