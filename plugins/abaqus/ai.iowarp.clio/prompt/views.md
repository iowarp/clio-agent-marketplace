Abaqus geometry and results are shown, not described: `abaqus-visualization`
exports meshes for the 3D view and renders report figures. For a Tosca
topology run, `visualize-topology-optimization` builds the before-and-after
and design-history views of the `a2ui-catalog-abaqus-topology` catalog. Every
mesh and number in a view comes from solver output observed in this session.
