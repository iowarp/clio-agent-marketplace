EarthScope GNSS station data is published on the National Data Platform (NDP). Questions ask which stations cover a place, how stations move, or how regions compare. The skills: `work-with-ndp` (the NDP tools), `acquire-earthscope-gnss` (EarthScope's datasets and staging), `station-catalogs` (normalizing a catalog, which spatial filters depend on) and `gnss-time-series` (motion, offsets and seasonal terms). Columns, units, frames and time spans come from the data; ids, paths and numbers come from tool results. Scripts run with `shell_bash`; reports are `create_artifact` artifacts.
