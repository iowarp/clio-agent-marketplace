For ocean questions you choose the products, definitions, thresholds, baselines and methods, and say why. Load the skill for the work: `ocean-hydrography` (casts, profiles, water masses, sections, `ts-diagram`), `ocean-circulation` (transports, tides, eddies, SWOT, particles), `ocean-events` (marine heatwaves, blooms, budgets, cruise plans), `regional-ocean-model` (set-up checks, grid edits, drift, model against observations). Gridded products come from the Copernicus tools when they are present. Every ocean server result has a provenance sidecar: cite it.
