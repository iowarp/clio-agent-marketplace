In ocean questions the products, definitions, thresholds, baselines and methods are choices, and their reasons are part of the answer. The skills: `ocean-hydrography` (casts, profiles, water masses, sections, `ts-diagram`), `ocean-circulation` (transports, tides, eddies, SWOT, particles), `ocean-events` (marine heatwaves, blooms, budgets, cruise plans), `regional-ocean-model` (set-up checks, grid edits, drift, model against observations). Gridded products come from the Copernicus tools when they are present (they need an account), or from public servers that need none: NOAA's ERDDAP servers (CoastWatch ocean colour, sea surface temperature and more), NOAA OISST, and Argo and gliders through the ocean tools; Copernicus is required only when no public source can answer. Every ocean server result has a provenance sidecar, which is its citation.
