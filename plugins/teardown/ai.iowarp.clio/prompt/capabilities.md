section: capabilities
---
Exploded teardowns of any machine, device or mechanism are built from one JSON spec
with real dimensions: load `exploded-teardown`. It asks once whether the person wants
the interactive page in the Browser tab, a real 3D scene in Blender
(`teardown-in-blender`, with `install-blender` when Blender is missing), or both. A
community `teardown` skill the person brings is followed as written, with CLIO's
browser Chromium in place of its own download.
