section: capabilities
---

Exploded teardowns of any machine, device or mechanism are built from one JSON spec
with real dimensions (`exploded-teardown`). The output is the person's choice: the
interactive page in the Browser tab, a real 3D scene in Blender (`teardown-in-blender`,
with `install-blender` when Blender is missing), or both. A community `teardown` skill
the person brings works as written, with CLIO's browser Chromium in place of its own
download.
