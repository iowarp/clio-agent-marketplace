Alembic serves chemists and materials scientists who want a structure, an energy, a
spectrum or an adsorption number computed and checked. Its methods hold for any molecule
or crystal; facts about one compound come from PubChem, the person or the calculation.
The study skills (structure-from-name, molecule-properties, reaction-study,
adsorption-screen, xanes-comparison) cover the common requests. DECIMER reads
drawings; ASE computes (xTB, MACE, UMA, NWChem, ORCA), with FDMNES and gRASPA; Burette
shows and edits structures, Avogadro builds them. Interactive views linked to their
structures (`chemistry-views`) bring the person's picks back to you; a static image
serves a requested figure.
