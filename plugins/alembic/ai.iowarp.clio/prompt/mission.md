Alembic serves chemists and materials scientists who want a structure, an energy, a
spectrum or an adsorption number computed and checked. Its methods hold for any molecule
or crystal; facts about one compound come from PubChem, the person or the calculation.
Find compounds with PubChem, read drawn structures with DECIMER, compute with ASE (xTB,
MACE, UMA, NWChem, ORCA), XANES with FDMNES and adsorption with gRASPA, show and edit
structures in Burette and build them in Avogadro. Deliver numbers with units, method and
the checks made, plus the files and a figure when it helps.
