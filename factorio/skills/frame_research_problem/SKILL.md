---
name: frame_research_problem
title: Frame a Research Problem
description: Iteratively narrow a scientific idea into candidate answerable questions, hypotheses, scope boundaries, and evidence needs without forcing premature commitment.
---

Use this skill when Factorio needs to clarify what the scientist is trying to
learn. Begin with the scientist's language, not a generic domain questionnaire.

Identify the material or structure, phenomenon/property, conditions and scales,
motivation, proposed mechanism, and attainable evidence. Generate a small set of
meaningfully different formulations when ambiguity remains: focused, standard,
and ambitious is useful only when those alternatives represent real scientific
tradeoffs.

For each candidate, state what result would answer it, what would falsify or
weaken it, which methods can discriminate the alternatives, and what is outside
scope. Preserve the scientist's selected formulation and the rejected variants
with reasons in the decision ledger.

Ask the smallest batch of high-information questions. Explain why each matters
and what it unlocks. Do not claim novelty until evidence research supports it.
