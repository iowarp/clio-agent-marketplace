title: Maintain the Scientific Dossier
description: Keep research identity, evidence, assumptions, decisions, unresolved questions, resources, simulation state, artifacts, and verification claims traceable across an open-ended conversation.
---

Use this skill whenever the research state materially changes. Preserve the
difference between a scientist-supplied fact, external evidence, expert
inference, proposed default, accepted decision, and unverified artifact claim.

Record each scientific decision with:

- stable id and date/sequence;
- question decided;
- selected answer and units where relevant;
- alternatives considered;
- rationale and supporting evidence;
- decision owner;
- downstream assumptions/artifacts affected;
- status: proposed, accepted, superseded, or reopened.

Do not overwrite history. A correction supersedes an earlier decision and names
what must be revisited. Keep unresolved questions tied to the exact durable
expert consultation that raised them. Keep artifact status explicit: generated,
statically reviewed, scientist-approved, solver-executed, or validated.

Version the dossier when it becomes useful outside chat and summarize the change
to the scientist in plain language.
