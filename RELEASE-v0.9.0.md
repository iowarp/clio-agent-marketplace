CLIO Marketplace 0.9.0 ships the default agents and plugins for CLIO 0.9.8. It adds computational chemistry, ocean and weather science, National Data Platform data access across organizations, electronics and simulation, and connectors that carry their own skills.

### Computational chemistry
The Alembic agent and its plugins work with ASE (xTB, MACE, NWChem), PubChem, Burette and Avogadro views, FDMNES XANES and gRASPA, with the computational-chemistry method pack.

### Ocean and weather
Ocean and weather plugins cover hydrography, transport, eddies, marine heatwaves, regional MOM6 setup, ensembles, AI forecasts and climate evaluation. Each tool output names the view a scientist checks it in.

### National Data Platform
NDP work reads data through CLIO's connected sources, with windowed reads, storage release and skills that find and join datasets across organizations. New wildfire, geospatial and landscape plugins carry the domain methods.

### Electronics, simulation and visualization
KiCad board design, SPICE circuit simulation, ParaView, IBM Quantum and Home Assistant plugins, each following its connector.

## Changed
Plugins follow the connectors they use, and the standard agent's skills and fragments were tightened from qualification runs.
